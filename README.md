**Savora – Your Financial Compass**

Savora is an intelligent personal finance management platform that helps users track expenses, manage investments, and plan financial goals efficiently.

**Features**

Expense tracking to monitor daily spending.  
Financial dashboard with summarized insights.  
Insurance payment tracking with due reminders.  
SIP investment tracker to manage monthly investments.  
Loan and EMI management for better financial planning.  
Savings division to allocate monthly income into categories.  
CSV import for transaction data.  
Financial predictions and insights.

**Tech Stack**

Frontend – React  
Backend – Supabase
